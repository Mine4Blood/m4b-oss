About Mine4Blood

Learn about who we are, what drives us, and why we believe in open source.

Who We Are

Mine4Blood is a community-driven project focused on building open source tools and resources. Our team is made up of passionate developers and contributors who believe that software should be transparent, accessible, and built together.

Our Values

Transparency — All of our code is publicly available. We develop in the open so anyone can see, review, and contribute to our work.

Community — We believe the best software is built together. Our community forum is the heart of our project, where ideas are shared and decisions are made collectively.

Quality — We strive to write clean, well-documented, and maintainable code. Every contribution goes through peer review to ensure high standards.

Get Involved

Whether you are a developer, designer, or just curious — there are many ways to contribute. Check out our projects, join the forum, or browse our documentation to get started.
